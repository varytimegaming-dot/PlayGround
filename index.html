How to play

Race all four of your tokens from the home yard, around the board, and up your color's home lane.

  1. Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the board. A six earns another roll.
  2. Tap a highlighted token to move it. You need an exact roll to reach home.
  3. Land on a rival to send them back to their yard. Star tiles are safe.
  4. Finish all four tokens before the other players to win.

You're playing against three friendly computer opponents. Good luck!
